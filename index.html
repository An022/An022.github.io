02 / THE PATH

I didn't leave audit behind.
I brought it into tech.

The roles changed. The habit of tracing evidence,
risk, and systems became the connecting thread.

DISCOVER / 01

PwC audit trained the questions.

Across complex audit and advisory work, I learned to trace evidence, test controls, notice what does not reconcile, and explain why a finding matters to the people making decisions.

DESIGN / 02

Automation revealed a different path.

Using Python and Excel to modernize audit testing showed me that recurring friction was often a systems problem. I became more interested in redesigning the workflow than working around it.

BUILD / 03

NYU computer science added the tools.

An M.S. in Computer Science gave me the engineering foundation to turn that instinct into APIs, data models, workflows, and user-facing products.

DELIVER / 04

Now I build where both sides matter.

From financial risk workflows to learning products, I translate complex requirements into production systems that are auditable, scalable, and useful to the people operating them.
