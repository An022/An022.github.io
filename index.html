CHAPTER ONE · TAIWAN

The story began with numbers—and one question too many.

I grew up in Taiwan and studied accounting because I liked structure. But the final number was never the most interesting part.

What held my attention was the story underneath it: why it moved, what it left out, and who needed to understand it.

“If the story is true, the evidence should know it too.”

CHAPTER TWO · PwC

At PwC, curiosity learned to become evidence.

As a senior auditor in Taipei, I learned to stay patient inside complicated systems: follow the trail, test the control, and explain why an exception mattered.

Then I led a digital pilot and a cross-team effort to automate the work. The lesson was simple: careful thinking becomes more powerful when everyone can use it.

CHAPTER FOUR · THE MIMIR

The questions became a product people could use.

At The Mimir, I helped build a language-learning platform from scratch. It grew into a real place where thousands of learners practiced, listened, and tried again.

I improved performance and built AI-assisted speaking feedback—technology mattered most when it made the next attempt feel easier.

CHAPTER FIVE · BOC

Then the systems became larger, quieter, and more consequential.

At BOC, I moved nightly loan processing from SSIS into Python, FastAPI, Airflow, and Pandas—connecting different sources into one dependable flow.

On-call work taught me that reliability is not a diagram. It is a promise kept overnight, while everyone else is asleep.

5+ data sources30K+ records daily60% faster resolution

EPILOGUE · STILL BUILDING

The ending is still being written.

From Taiwan to New York, from audit to software, the thread was curiosity—not reinvention.

Today I work where business judgment and engineering meet. I trace the evidence, ask one more why, and turn recurring friction into systems people can trust.

Applybook is one of those experiments: a calmer workspace for a job search that keeps changing.
